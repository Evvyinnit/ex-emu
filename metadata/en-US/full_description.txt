Ex Emu is a fully working all-in-one game emulator for Android.

- Java (J2ME) games: load .jar/.jad files, virtual keyboard, per-game key mapping and scaling, saves, Mascot Capsule 3D support
- 30+ console systems via libretro cores: NES, SNES, GB/GBC/GBA, NDS, 3DS, N64, Genesis, Master System, Game Gear, Sega CD, PS1, PSP, PS2, FBNeo, MAME, Atari 2600/7800/Lynx, PC Engine, Neo Geo, DOS and more
- Cores are downloaded on demand and cached on your device

Developed by Evvyinnit.
